## Recovery boundary

The adapter retains the 45/150/300-second startup gates, at most one first-trial
startup-failure recovery restart, zero daily power recovery and no resident
monitor. It retains daily PR28 250 ms and the WindowServer exclusion. Extra GPU
pressure tests are outside this observation scope.

Recovery preflights the exact current R2a hashes and every restore target. It
restores only the accepted R1b executable, the **current R2a-compatible** startup
job pair and its own watcher/pending/hold state. It cannot restore the old R1a
kernel, VBL-only accelerator or old cache through the inherited recovery map.
Restoration itself requests no power. The inherited one-use journal controls
any subsequent recovery restart, after independent accepted-parent disk,
cache, AML, plugin, security and job verification. No healthy observer startup
is automatically rolled back. This is tested source behavior, not an executed
native recovery or firmware-level protection against every pre-launch hang.

Normal construction checks the pinned parent contract without constructing an
extra independent recovery verifier on its timed startup path. That verifier
is constructed only during recovery. Importing the module installs nothing;
it has no installer or ordinary-restart dispatcher.
